# Olahtek Services & Solutions

## Services
Olahtek provides three core digital services for businesses:
- Web Development
- AI-Powered Systems
- Business Process Automation

We build practical digital solutions based on each client's business needs. Services can be delivered individually or combined into a custom solution.

Olahtek is an independent, engineering-led technology studio. Every project is assessed based on requirements, technical feasibility, scope, and available resources.

We design and develop websites that help businesses establish their online presence and present their products, services, and information.

### What we build
- Business and company websites.
- Service-based websites.
- Landing pages.
- Product and portfolio websites.
- Responsive websites for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Contact and inquiry forms.
- Custom web applications.
- Websites integrated with AI systems or business automations.

### Typical deliverables
Depending on the project:

### Choosing the right solution
- Business website: for businesses that need an online presence.
- Landing page: for a specific service, product, or campaign.
- Custom web application: for businesses requiring dashboards, user accounts, databases, or interactive workflows.
- Website with integrations: for businesses that want their website connected to other tools or systems.

## AI-Powered Systems
We build or integrate AI capabilities into websites, applications, and business workflows when they provide practical value.

### What we build
- AI assistants for defined business tasks.
- AI-powered customer inquiry support.
- Document extraction and processing.
- Document classification.
- Knowledge search and question-answering systems.
- AI-powered information organization.
- AI features integrated into websites and applications.
- AI-assisted business workflows.

### What AI can help with
AI may help businesses process information, search documents, classify data, summarize inquiries, or assist with repetitive information-related tasks.

AI is not suitable for every problem. A simpler rule-based system may be more reliable for predictable tasks.

### Important limitations
- AI outputs may be incorrect or incomplete.
- AI accuracy depends on the use case and available data.
- Human review may be required for important decisions.
- External AI services may have usage costs or availability limits.
- AI does not guarantee complete automation or business results.

AI model subscriptions, API usage, hosting, data preparation, and maintenance may involve additional costs.

## Business Process Automation
We connect business tools and automate selected repetitive workflows to reduce manual handling.

### What we automate
- Customer inquiry collection and routing.
- Lead follow-up reminders.
- Notifications and confirmation messages.
- Spreadsheet and database updates.
- CRM integrations.
- Scheduled reports and data processing.
- Data synchronization between supported applications.
- Form submissions connected to business workflows.
- AI-assisted steps within automation workflows.

### Common examples
- A website inquiry form automatically creates a lead record.
- A new lead triggers a notification to the responsible staff member.
- A booking confirmation triggers a follow-up message.
- Spreadsheet updates synchronize with another business system.
- A document is processed and selected information is sent for review.

Automation is most suitable for repetitive tasks with clear rules. Exceptions and sensitive decisions may still require human involvement.


# Olahtek — Delivery & Handover Policy

## 1. About Delivery

Olahtek provides digital services, including website development, custom applications, AI-powered systems, and business process automation.

Since Olahtek delivers digital products and services, projects are generally delivered electronically rather than through physical shipping.

Delivery methods and timelines depend on the project scope, agreed milestones, customer requirements, and any third-party services involved.

## 2. What Does Delivery Include?

Depending on the project agreement, delivery may include:

- A completed website or web application
- A deployed application or system
- Configured automation workflows
- AI-powered features or assistants
- Source code or project files, if included in the agreement
- Documentation or basic usage instructions
- A walkthrough or handover session, if agreed
- Access details or account transfer instructions, where applicable

Only deliverables included in the approved project scope should be promised.

## 3. How Are Digital Projects Delivered?

Olahtek may deliver projects through one or more of the following methods:

- **Live deployment:** The website or application is made available through an agreed domain or hosting environment.
- **Staging environment:** The customer reviews the project in a test environment before final deployment.
- **File handover:** Project files, documents, or source code are provided through an agreed file-sharing method.
- **Platform handover:** Workflows or configurations are transferred through the relevant platform, where supported.
- **Account or access transfer:** Ownership or access is provided according to the project agreement and platform capabilities.

The delivery method will be confirmed during project planning or before handover.

## 4. Project Delivery Timeline

### When will my project be delivered?

The estimated delivery timeline depends on:
- Project complexity and scope
- Required features and integrations
- Availability of content, assets, and customer feedback
- Testing and revisions
- Third-party platform or service dependencies

Olahtek will provide an estimated timeline after reviewing the project requirements.

The assistant must not invent or guarantee a delivery date that has not been confirmed by the responsible person.

### What can delay delivery?

A project timeline may be affected by:
- Changes to the original requirements
- Delayed customer feedback or approvals
- Missing content, credentials, or access
- Third-party service issues
- Additional features or integration complexity
- Unexpected technical problems discovered during development

If a delay is identified, the responsible person should communicate the updated timeline and next steps.

## 5. Customer Responsibilities Before Delivery

To help keep the project on schedule, customers may need to provide:

- Approved project requirements
- Website text, images, branding, or other content
- Access to relevant platforms or services
- Domain and hosting details, if customer-owned
- API access or integration permissions, where required
- Feedback and approvals at agreed milestones

Customers should share credentials through a secure method, not through ordinary chat.

Delays in providing required information or approvals may affect the delivery schedule.

## 6. Testing and Approval

Before final handover, Olahtek may conduct testing based on the agreed project scope.

Depending on the project, testing may include:
- Core functionality
- Forms and user interactions
- Responsive layout
- API and integration behavior
- Automation triggers and actions
- AI feature behavior and error handling
- Basic deployment checks

Customers may be asked to review the project and provide feedback.

Any revision or correction process will follow the terms agreed for the project. Requests that introduce new functionality or change approved requirements may be treated as additional work.

## 7. Deployment and Hosting

A project may be deployed to:
- Customer-owned hosting or infrastructure
- Hosting arranged by Olahtek, if agreed
- A third-party platform or cloud service
- A staging or testing environment

Hosting, domain registration, platform subscriptions, API usage, and other recurring costs are not automatically included in the development fee.

The responsible person should confirm:
- Who owns and manages the hosting account
- Who pays recurring service fees
- Whether deployment is included
- Whether ongoing maintenance is included
- What access or documentation will be handed over

## 8. Final Handover

At the end of a project, the handover may include the deliverables specified in the agreement.

Before handover, Olahtek should confirm:
- The agreed deliverables are ready
- Required testing has been completed
- Customer review or approval has been obtained, where applicable
- Access and ownership arrangements are clear
- Documentation or walkthroughs included in the scope are provided
- Any outstanding payments or conditions are addressed according to the agreement

Do not claim that a project has been delivered, deployed, or transferred until the responsible person confirms completion.

## 9. Post-Delivery Support

After delivery, support depends on the project agreement.

Post-delivery support may include:
- Assistance with the delivered system
- Fixes for issues covered by agreed warranty terms
- Guidance on using the delivered features
- Maintenance or monitoring, if separately arranged

New features, major changes, ongoing maintenance, and third-party service issues are not automatically included in project delivery.

For warranty or refund questions, refer to the **Olahtek Returns, Refunds & Warranty Policy** and the individual project agreement.

## 10. Delivery Issues

If a customer reports that a deliverable is missing, inaccessible, or not functioning as expected, the assistant should ask for:

- Customer or business name
- Project name
- Which deliverable or feature is affected
- A description of the issue
- Relevant screenshots or error messages
- When the issue was noticed

The team should verify the project status and determine whether the issue relates to delivery, access, a technical defect, or a request outside the agreed scope.

Do not ask customers to share passwords, API keys, or other confidential credentials in chat.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Olahtek ship physical products?

No. Olahtek primarily provides digital services and deliverables. Physical shipping is not part of the standard service offering.

### Will I receive my website or application online?

Deployment may be included depending on the project agreement. The hosting environment, domain, and deployment responsibilities should be confirmed before development or handover.

### Will I receive the source code?

Source code or project files are provided when included in the agreed project scope. The assistant should not promise source-code transfer without checking the agreement.

### Can I request changes before delivery?

Customers may provide feedback during agreed review stages. Whether a request is included depends on the project scope and revision terms. New features or substantial changes may require a separate estimate.

### What happens if I need help after delivery?

The available support depends on the project’s warranty or maintenance terms. The assistant should refer to the project agreement or escalate the request for confirmation.

## 12. AI Assistant Rules

The assistant must:

- Explain that Olahtek delivers digital services, not physical shipments.
- Provide delivery estimates only when confirmed.
- Distinguish between project completion, deployment, and final handover.
- Never promise hosting, domains, source code, maintenance, or documentation unless included in scope.
- Avoid claiming a project is complete or transferred without confirmation.
- Ask concise questions when delivery status or project details are unclear.
- Escalate timeline disputes, access problems, and unclear delivery commitments.

## 13. Internal Policy Notes

Before using this as a fixed customer-facing policy, Olahtek should confirm:

- Standard project milestones and delivery process
- Whether staging and deployment are included by default
- Source-code ownership and transfer terms
- Hosting and domain responsibilities
- Customer review and approval process
- Revision limits
- Handover documentation and training
- Post-delivery support arrangements

Until these details are confirmed, the assistant must refer to the individual project agreement and avoid inventing commitments.

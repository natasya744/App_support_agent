
# Olahtek — Returns, Refunds & Warranty Policy

## 1. About This Policy

Olahtek provides digital services such as website development, custom applications, AI-powered systems, and business process automation.

Because these services involve project planning, development time, technical work, and sometimes third-party services, requests for refunds, cancellations, warranty support, or revisions are reviewed based on the agreed project scope and terms.

This knowledge base helps answer common customer questions. It does not replace the terms agreed upon for an individual project.

## 2. Refunds

### Can customers request a refund?

Customers may request a refund by contacting Olahtek and explaining the reason for their request.

Refund eligibility depends on:
- The project agreement and payment terms
- Work already completed
- Any deliverables already provided
- Costs already incurred for third-party services or resources
- The reason for the refund request

A refund is not automatically guaranteed simply because a customer changes their mind after work has started.

### What if the project has not started?

If development has not started, the request may be reviewed based on the agreed payment and cancellation terms.

Do not promise a full refund unless the responsible person has confirmed that the project is eligible.

### What if work has already started?

If work has started, Olahtek will review the completed work, project status, and applicable agreement before determining whether any refund is possible.

### What if the customer is dissatisfied with the result?

Olahtek will first review the concern against the agreed requirements and deliverables.

If the issue is within the agreed scope, the team may assess whether a correction or revision is appropriate. A refund should not be promised before the project has been reviewed.

## 3. Project Cancellation

### Can a customer cancel a project?

Customers may request cancellation by contacting Olahtek.

The outcome depends on:
- The project’s current stage
- Work already completed
- Payments made
- Any non-refundable third-party costs
- The cancellation terms agreed upon before work began

### What happens after cancellation?

Olahtek will review the project status and explain:
- What work has been completed
- Which deliverables, if any, can be handed over
- Whether any remaining payment is due
- Whether any refund is applicable under the agreement

Do not confirm cancellation, refund amounts, or final handover arrangements without approval from the responsible person.

## 4. Warranty and Post-Delivery Support

### Does Olahtek provide a warranty?

Warranty or post-delivery support may be included in a project when it is stated in the project agreement or proposal.

The applicable support period, covered issues, and response process depend on the specific project terms.

If the customer asks about warranty coverage and the agreement is not available, explain that the team needs to verify the project terms before confirming.

### What issues may be covered?

Where warranty support has been agreed, it may cover defects that cause the delivered system to fail to perform according to the approved requirements.

Examples may include:
- A delivered feature not functioning as specified
- A reproducible software bug in the agreed scope
- A technical issue caused by Olahtek’s implementation

Coverage must be assessed against the actual agreement and project requirements.

### What is generally not covered?

Unless otherwise agreed, warranty support does not automatically include:
- New features or functionality
- Changes to the original requirements
- Design or content changes requested after approval
- Problems caused by customer modifications or third-party changes
- Issues caused by hosting providers, external APIs, plugins, or other services outside Olahtek’s control
- Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, or content updates
- Training or additional implementation work beyond the agreed scope

These requests may be handled as separate paid work, subject to review and approval.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Some projects may rely on external platforms, hosting providers, APIs, AI models, plugins, or other third-party services.

Olahtek cannot guarantee the availability, pricing, performance, or continued operation of services controlled by third parties.

If a problem appears to be caused by a third-party service, the team will review the issue and explain what can reasonably be done.

Third-party subscription fees and usage charges are governed by the relevant provider’s terms and are not automatically refundable by Olahtek.

## 6. How to Request Support or a Refund

Customers should contact Olahtek through the official communication channel used for their project and provide:

- Customer or business name
- Project name or description
- The issue or request
- When the issue occurred
- Screenshots, error messages, or relevant details
- What outcome they are requesting

Customers should not send passwords, API keys, payment card details, or other confidential credentials through ordinary chat.

Olahtek may request additional information to understand and assess the issue.

## 7. Support Review Process

When a request is received, Olahtek will:

1. Identify the customer and project.
2. Review the agreed scope, payment terms, and support conditions.
3. Determine whether the request concerns a defect, a change request, a third-party issue, cancellation, or refund.
4. Explain the next steps to the customer.
5. Escalate requests requiring approval before confirming an outcome.

Do not claim that a refund, repair, cancellation, or warranty claim has been approved until the responsible person confirms it.

## 8. Response Guidance for the AI Assistant

The assistant should:

- Ask for the project name and a brief description of the issue when needed.
- Explain that refund and warranty eligibility depends on the project agreement.
- Avoid promising refunds, free repairs, fixed support periods, or guaranteed response times.
- Distinguish between a defect in agreed functionality and a request for new features.
- Escalate requests involving refunds, disputes, cancellation, legal concerns, or unclear warranty terms.
- Never claim that a request has been processed or approved unless confirmation is available.
- Keep responses clear, professional, and factual.

## 9. Suggested Customer Response

**For a refund request:**

“Thank you for contacting Olahtek. Refund eligibility depends on the project agreement and the work completed so far. Could you share your project name and the reason for your request? We’ll review the details and confirm the next steps.”

**For a warranty or technical issue:**

“Thank you for letting us know. Please share your project name, a description of the issue, and any relevant screenshots or error messages. We’ll review the issue against the agreed project scope and support terms.”

**For a request outside the original scope:**

“Thank you for sharing your request. This appears to involve a change or additional functionality beyond the original project scope. We’ll review the requirements and confirm whether it can be handled as a separate task.”

## 10. Internal Policy Notes

The following details must be confirmed by Olahtek before being communicated as fixed policy:

- Whether deposits are refundable
- Cancellation terms and any applicable fees
- Whether a standard warranty period is offered
- The start and end date of warranty coverage
- The response and resolution process during warranty
- Whether maintenance plans are available
- The official channel for support and refund requests
- Any applicable consumer or legal requirements

Until these details are confirmed, the assistant must refer to the individual project agreement and escalate policy-specific questions.
